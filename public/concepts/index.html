Paperclip Hub — two directions

The current hub reads busy and “AI-generated”: newspaper tropes (volume markers, § ii — the directory, tickers, stamps), a warm ruled-paper texture, and serif flourishes competing for attention. These two concepts strip that back toward the new Paperclip design language — monochrome, type-forward, whitespace-driven, calm — the direction set by paperclip.ing and clawhub.ai. Pick one to take to a full implementation.

Concept A

Registry

Directory-first and utilitarian, in the clawhub / Linear vein. Left-aligned hero, featured + trending split, dense but calm card grid.

  • Best when the primary job is browse & install fast.
  • One indigo accent, everything else greyscale.
  • Sidebar keeps “trending” always visible.

 Concept B

Studio

Product-forward and branded, in the paperclip.ing vein. Centered hero, a 3-step “how it works”, then the directory, closing on a dark CTA band.

  • Best when the hub also has to sell the platform.
  • Pure monochrome (black ink), no color accent.
  • More marketing surface above the grid.

What changes, either way

Textureruled-paper gradient + warm cream → flat white / near-white
TypeInstrument Serif italics + editorial voice → one sans (Geist), tight tracking, clear hierarchy
Decorationvolume markers, § numerals, tickers, stamps → removed — whitespace does the work
Colorred accent + multiple warm tones → greyscale + at most one restrained accent
Cardsheavy rules & borders everywhere → light 1px lines, space + subtle shadow on hover
Kept intactsearch, category filters, sort, featured, grid, trending top-5, submit CTA, stats, footer

Static mockups with representative data — not the production build. Once a direction is chosen, it becomes the real Next.js homepage + a rewrite of hub.css, preserving all current functionality.
